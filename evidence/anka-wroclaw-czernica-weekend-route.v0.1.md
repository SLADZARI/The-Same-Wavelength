# ANKA — Wrocław → Czernica Family Weekend Route v0.1

## 1. Product frame

- Boat: ANKA, classic Dutch steel motor cruiser.
- Working dimensions: approx. 10.0 m × 2.9 m, draft approx. 0.70 m.
- Layout assumption used for the product: forward private cabin + aft private cabin + convertible salon.
- Crew: 2 hosts / crew.
- Guest target: one family, normally 2 adults + 1–2 children.
- Total persons in the working scenario: 4–6.
- Product duration: Friday 17:00 to Sunday approx. 16:00–17:00.
- Commercial target price: 4,500 PLN per family / whole boat.
- Product positioning: private family river weekend, not bareboat rental.

Fact labels:
- Boat layout with aft cabin: USER-CONFIRMED.
- Route distances from official Odra kilometre markers: FACT where cited.
- Speed, current and fuel figures: ESTIMATE until a calibration trip is completed.
- Overnight permission at Zimowisko Barek II and guest berth availability in Czernica: UNKNOWN until confirmed.

## 2. Route summary

Working route:

Friday:
Marina Osobowice / Osobowice I → Wrocław city leg toward Śluza Mieszczańska → return past Osobowice → Zimowisko Barek Osobowice II.

Saturday:
Zimowisko Barek II → Różanka → Zacisze → Bartoszowice → Janowice → Przystań Wodna Czernica.

Sunday:
Przystań Wodna Czernica → Janowice → Bartoszowice → Zacisze → Różanka → Marina Osobowice.

## 4. Locks

- Różanka

- Bartoszowice

Working assumption:
- 4 lock passages on Saturday.
- 4 lock passages on Sunday.

Current official operating hours and navigation notices must be checked before each trip. Wody Polskie notes that route timing depends on hydrology, navigation notices, lock availability and traffic.

## 5. Overnight plan

### Night 1 — Zimowisko Barek Osobowice II

Preferred experiential version:
- enter the former barge wintering basin at approximately km 259;
- engine off;
- quiet / still-water overnight feeling close to Wrocław.

Verified navigation fact:
Wody Polskie describes the basin as a place that can be entered for rest and notes standing water.

Open gate before commercial use:
- verify that overnight commercial stay with paying guests is permitted;
- verify safe mooring method, depth, shore access and emergency egress.

Fallback:
Marina Osobowice.

### Night 2 — Przystań Wodna Czernica

Destination overnight after the main upstream leg.

Evidence:
- Czernica is the home port of a commercial SunCamper 36 operation;
- current local operator routes include Czernica toward Janowice and toward Wrocław.

Open gate:
confirm a guest berth suitable for ANKA approximately 10.0 × 2.9 m and permission for a commercial overnight stay.

## 8. Family experience / shore stops

The route is intentionally not designed as a stop-every-hour excursion.

Planned shore rhythm:
- Friday: board at Osobowice, then stay aboard.
- Saturday: primary shore exit at Czernica.
- Sunday: final shore exit at Osobowice.

Optional Wrocław stop near a passenger / tourist landing may be added only after confirming that a 10 m commercial cruiser can legally and safely berth there.

## 9. Operational go / no-go gates

Before selling or operating the route:

1. ANKA commercial-use registration / legal status confirmed.
2. Required technical certificate / survey completed.
3. Commercial insurance explicitly covers paying passengers and overnight use.
4. Maximum permitted persons confirmed in registration / technical documents.
5. Current navigation notices checked.
6. Transit depth compatible with ANKA draft and safety margin.
7. Lock operating status checked.
8. Weather and current acceptable.
9. Night 1 location confirmed or fallback Marina Osobowice reserved.
10. Czernica guest berth confirmed.
11. Black-water / toilet arrangement verified for commercial use.
12. Emergency contact and shore evacuation points defined.

Any failed navigation, berth, legal or safety gate converts the route to HOLD or to an approved fallback itinerary.
